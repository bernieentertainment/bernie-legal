01

Introduction

Bernie Entertainment Inc. ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Word Up! mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App.

By accessing or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with these terms, please do not access the App.

02

Information We Collect

Account & Profile Information

When you create an account or use certain features, we may collect:

  • Display name or username
  • Email address (for account registration and recovery)
  • Profile preferences and in-app settings

Purchase & Subscription Information

Transactions are processed through Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We do not directly collect or store your payment card details. Subscription and purchase data is processed by RevenueCat, our subscription management provider, which validates purchases and manages your entitlements on our behalf. We receive:

  • Purchase receipt identifiers
  • Type of purchase and entitlements unlocked
  • Transaction timestamps
  • Subscription status (active, expired, trial)

Advertising Data (Google AdMob)

The App displays advertisements to users who have not purchased an ad-free experience. AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android)
  • Device type, operating system, and language
  • IP address and approximate location (city/region level)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)
Note: AdMob's data collection is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You may opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings at any time.

Analytics & Crash Data

We use analytics and crash-reporting services to understand App usage and identify technical issues. This may include:

  • App usage events (screens visited, features used, session duration)
  • Crash logs and error reports
  • Device identifiers, OS version, and app version
  • General geographic region (country or region level)

This data is collected in aggregate or pseudonymous form and is used solely to improve performance and user experience.

03

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the App and its features
  • Process and fulfill in-app purchases and restore entitlements
  • Serve advertisements and measure ad performance (via AdMob)
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve the App
  • Diagnose and fix technical issues and crashes
  • Respond to support requests or inquiries
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations
04

Sharing of Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your data with the following categories of third parties:

  • RevenueCat — for subscription management, purchase validation, and entitlement tracking
  • Google AdMob — for serving and measuring advertisements
  • Analytics providers — for crash reporting and usage analytics (e.g., Firebase)
  • Apple / Google — for in-app purchase verification and app distribution
  • Legal authorities — if required by law, court order, or to protect the safety of our users or the public

All third-party service providers are contractually required to handle your data in compliance with applicable privacy laws.

05

Children's Privacy

Word Up! is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected such information, we will delete it promptly.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at info@bernieentertainment.com.

06

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the App's services. Analytics and crash data are typically retained in aggregate for up to 24 months.

You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the address below.

07

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Withdraw consent for data collection where consent is the legal basis
  • Opt out of personalized advertising through your device's advertising settings
Québec residents (Loi 25 / Act 25): In accordance with Quebec's Act Respecting the Protection of Personal Information in the Private Sector, you have the right to be informed about the collection, use, and disclosure of your personal information, and to request access or correction.
European users (GDPR): If you are located in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom, you have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation, including the right to data portability and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Our legal basis for processing your data is generally your consent (for advertising and analytics) or our legitimate interest in operating and improving the App.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@bernieentertainment.com.
